VelozReady is an app and service owned and operated by Sahar Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Sahar Ventures LLC," "we," "us," or "our"). In these notices, "VelozReady" also means Sahar Ventures LLC providing the VelozReady app and service. Privacy contact: support@velozready.com.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information VelozReady collects through its iPhone app, website, and related services (the "Service"), how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. The Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice adds details for health-related information. The Team Sharing Notice explains what coaches can see. VelozReady is for people in the United States age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Age: at sign-up you enter your birth month and year. The app uses them on your device to confirm you are at least 13 and to work out your age range. We store only the age range (13–15, 16–17, or 18+) and the date it next changes, never your birth month or year.
  • Profile: display name, role (athlete or coach), sport, event focus, training level, graduation year, school or organization name, staff title (coaches), goals, personal records (PRs), athlete code, and an optional profile photo. Your profile photo is not shown to anyone else at this time.
  • Practice and race check-ins: date, type, sport, event, workout or meet, distance, duration, effort (practices), race time (races), one "How did you feel?" or "How did it go?" answer (Great, OK, or Rough), and notes. Earlier check-ins may also include a photo or watch screenshot; VelozReady no longer accepts new ones, and you can still view or delete the old ones. Check-ins saved before November 13, 2026 may also include the more detailed answers the app used to ask (legs, breathing, energy, sleep, fueling, hydration, pain, and race segments); they stay in your history until you edit or delete them. VelozReady no longer asks about pain.
  • Coach verification (coaches only): organization type, name, city, state, and website; a verification email; a public staff-directory link; and notes you submit for review.
  • Team board: posts you write or edit (team chat, and announcements and workouts if you are a coach or alumni), reactions, posts you pin, reports you make, and people you block.
  • Team settings (coaches): team calendar events, a team logo uploaded by the head coach, the team chat on/off setting, roster names a coach gives athletes on their roster (seen only by that team's coaches), and the email addresses coaches enter to invite assistant coaches or alumni.
  • PR celebrations: your choice, per team, to let coaches and alumni post your new PRs on the team board.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to support@velozready.com.

Information created when you use the Service

  • Readiness information: a readiness score (0–100) and band that the app calculates on your iPhone when you save a practice (see below), plus trends built from those scores.
  • Team records: teams you request to join or belong to, invitations used, approvals, removals, and your sharing choices for each team.
  • Consent records: which version of each notice you accepted, when, how, your age range at the time, and any withdrawal.
  • Coach records: practice and meet information coaches on your team record about you, such as attendance, workout groups, times and splits, and meet entries. See the Team Sharing Notice, Section 3C.
  • Board activity: which announcements and workouts you have seen (teammates see only a count, such as "Seen by 3") and when you last opened each team's board (used for the unread dot).
  • Security records: sign-in activity, request counts used to prevent abuse (rate limits), and records needed to investigate misuse.

Information from Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, VelozReady reads heart-rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, heart rate, sleep, walking and running distance, and workouts from Apple Health on your iPhone. The app also works out your 30-day averages for resting heart rate and HRV on your iPhone, and uses today's sleep, resting heart rate, and HRV compared with those averages to calculate the readiness score for a practice you log that day. Only the resulting score is saved with the practice. The Apple Health values and averages used for it are not. Raw Apple Health samples stay on your iPhone. VelozReady sends a daily summary to our servers only for the categories you have shared with at least one team. Those summaries include the date, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep hours, distance, workout count, and latest workout details. If you are not sharing a Health category with any team, no Apple Health data for it leaves your iPhone. VelozReady never writes to Apple Health.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting and content-delivery providers automatically receive your IP address, device and browser type, operating system, and app version when your device connects to the Service. VelozReady does not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or precise location (GPS).

The app also keeps some information on your device, such as your sign-in session, recent history for faster loading, a pending invitation code, which notifications you have seen or dismissed, and your answer to the Apple Health prompt. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Your calendar. "Add to my calendar" opens iPhone's own New Event screen with the event filled in. You review it and tap Add. VelozReady never reads or changes your calendar.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure your account and confirm your age range;
  • save your training history and calculate readiness feedback and trends;
  • run team invitations and memberships and show coaches only what you choose to share;
  • run each team's board, calendar, logo, and team tiles, and help coaches moderate the board;
  • verify coaches' claimed organizations;
  • read Apple Health data you approve and sync daily summaries you share with a team;
  • keep records of your consent and choices;
  • respond to your requests and messages;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents and enforce our Terms; and
  • comply with law.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use identifiable health or training information to train artificial-intelligence models.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • With coaches, at your direction. A coach on a team you joined sees only the categories you turn on for that team, and only while you remain on the team. Information a coach records about you at practice or meets (such as attendance and times) is visible to your team's coaches and to you, and to teammates and alumni only when a coach shares it with the team. See the Team Sharing Notice.
  • With your team, when you post or opt in. Your board posts and reactions are visible to everyone on that team, including coaches and alumni. If you turn on "Celebrate my PRs with the team," coaches and alumni may post your new PR (event, time, improvement, and meet) for the team to see. If you share race results with a team, your best 5K counts toward the team average 5K. Teammates and alumni see that average only when at least three runners count toward it, so no one's time can be worked out, and see a PRs-this-week count only from teammates who turned on PR celebrations. Alumni never see any athlete's shared practices, races, readiness, or Apple Health information.
  • With service providers that run the Service for us, who may use it only to provide their services to us:

- Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, server functions, and account emails), with data stored in the United States; - Apple (App Store distribution and Apple Health on your device); and - jsDelivr (delivers app code libraries; it receives your IP address and device information, not your account data).

  • For legal and safety reasons, when we believe in good faith it is required by law or legal process, or needed to protect someone's safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or enforce our Terms. This includes reporting suspected child exploitation to authorities.
  • In a business transfer. If Sahar Ventures LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may transfer under this policy. We will notify you before it becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

A coach-created team does not mean a school, college, or club has bought, approved, or controls VelozReady. VelozReady does not receive student education records from schools.

6. How we protect information

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest by our hosting provider, private file storage with time-limited links, server-side permission checks so coaches can reach only data an athlete shared with them, row-level database security, append-only consent records, request rate limits, and restricted administrator access. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires (see Section 10).

7. Your choices and rights

In the app you can:

  • edit your profile, delete any check-in, and remove your profile photo;
  • edit or delete your own board posts, report posts, and block people on the board;
  • turn PR celebrations on or off for each team;
  • turn automatic team sharing on or off, turn each team-sharing category on or off, or leave a team;
  • turn off VelozReady's access to Apple Health in the iPhone Settings app; and
  • delete your account and data permanently (Settings → Delete account and data).

By email to support@velozready.com you can ask us to:

  • confirm what personal or health information we have about you and who we have shared it with;
  • give you a copy of it in a portable format;
  • correct it;
  • delete it, including Apple Health summaries without deleting your account; or
  • withdraw a consent you gave.

We will verify your request by confirming it comes from the email address on your account. We respond within 45 days (we may extend once by 45 more days if reasonably necessary, and we will tell you why). If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying with the subject "Privacy Appeal." We will answer the appeal within 45 days. If you are still not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general. We will never charge you, deny service, or treat you differently for using these rights. You may use an authorized agent where your state's law allows. We will ask for proof of the agent's authority.

Parents and guardians may email support@velozready.com to report a suspected account of a child under 13 or a safety concern. A parent or guardian does not automatically get access to a teen's account.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: VelozReady does not track you across other websites or apps and does not sell or share information for targeted advertising, so there is nothing for these browser signals to turn off. We treat them as a request to opt out where state law requires.

8. Teens (ages 13–17)

Teen accounts get the same privacy protections as all accounts plus these: team access requires the athlete's own request and a coach's approval; nothing is shared with a coach until the athlete chooses to share, either once for every team that approves them or team by team; and teen information is never used for advertising or sold. On the team board there are no private or direct messages: everything is visible to the whole team and its coaches. The only adults on a team are its coaches and alumni, who must be 18 or older and are added by the head coach. Alumni never see any athlete's data. When an athlete's age range changes (for example, at 16 or 18), VelozReady updates it automatically once the stored date arrives. If we learn that a user is under 13, we will delete the account and its information.

9. Laws that apply

VelozReady is designed to comply with the U.S. privacy laws that apply to it, including:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). VelozReady does not allow users under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
  • FTC Act and FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 318). We describe our practices truthfully, and we will notify affected users, the Federal Trade Commission, and where required the media, of a breach of identifiable health information.
  • Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373), Nevada consumer health data law (NRS Chapter 603A), and Connecticut's consumer health data provisions. These laws protect consumer health data. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice is our consumer health data privacy policy under them. We collect health data only with your separate consent at sign-up, and we share it with a coach only when you choose to share it.
  • California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and, where it applies, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). California residents have the rights described in Section 7. We do not sell or share personal information, and we use sensitive personal information only to provide the Service.
  • Florida Information Protection Act (Fla. Stat. § 501.171) and other state data-breach laws. We will notify affected people of a qualifying breach within the time those laws require.
  • Other state privacy laws (for example in Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas, Virginia, and Florida's Digital Bill of Rights) give residents rights to access, correct, delete, and copy their data and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising, and profiling. We honor those requests for all U.S. users as described in Section 7, whether or not a particular law applies to us.

Laws that generally do not apply: VelozReady is not a health-care provider, so the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) does not cover information in VelozReady. VelozReady does not act on behalf of schools, so the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) does not cover it.

Apple Health rules. As Apple requires, we do not use Apple Health data for advertising, do not sell it, do not store it in iCloud, and share it with a coach only with your permission and only to support your training.

10. Data breaches

If we discover a breach involving your personal or health information, we will investigate promptly. We will notify you by email, and notify regulators where required, within the time the applicable law requires. The Florida Information Protection Act requires notice within 30 days, and the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule within 60 days. Our notice will tell you what happened and what you can do.

11. United States only

The Service is offered only in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. When required, the app will ask you to review and accept the new version. The version and effective date are shown at the top of this policy.
