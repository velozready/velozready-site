VelozReady Age Eligibility Notice

Version1.3
Last updatedNovember 14, 2026
Effective dateNovember 14, 2026

VelozReady is operated by Sahar Ventures LLC, doing business as VelozReady. Contact: support@velozready.com.

1. Who can use VelozReady

VelozReady is for people in the United States who are 13 or older. Children under 13 may not create or use an account. If you are 13 to 17, you may use VelozReady with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. When you create your account you confirm that you have that permission.

2. How we check your age

When you create an account, you enter your birth month and year. The app uses them on your device to:

  • confirm you are at least 13; and
  • place you in an age range: 13–15, 16–17, or 18+.

Because only the month is known, VelozReady counts each birthday milestone (13, 16, and 18) from the first day of the month after your birthday month. That way no one is placed in an older range too early.

We store only your age range and the date it next changes. We never store your birth month, year, or exact birthdate. When that date arrives, VelozReady moves your account to the next range automatically (13–15 to 16–17, and 16–17 to 18+). You don't need to do anything.

3. How age information is used

Your age range is used to confirm eligibility, apply age-appropriate protections, and record your age range with each consent you give. It is never used for advertising and never sold.

4. Be truthful

Do not enter a false birth month or year, and do not create an account for a child under 13. If we believe an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it and its information. If we believe an age range is wrong, we may ask you to confirm your age or suspend the account.

5. Teams

An invitation code or link does not automatically connect you to a coach. You send a request and a coach approves it. Nothing is shared until you choose to share, either once for every team that approves you, or team by team. See the Team Sharing Notice.

6. Parents and guardians

A parent or guardian can email support@velozready.com to report a suspected account of a child under 13 or a safety concern. We will respond as the law and safety require. A report does not automatically give the parent or guardian access to a teen's account.

7. Laws that apply

VelozReady does not allow users under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Teen accounts are protected by the privacy laws described in the Privacy Policy, Section 9.
