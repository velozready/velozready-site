VelozReady Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Version1.4
Last updatedNovember 16, 2026
Effective dateNovember 19, 2026

VelozReady is an app and service owned and operated by Sahar Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Sahar Ventures LLC," "we," "us," or "our"). In these notices, "VelozReady" also means Sahar Ventures LLC providing the VelozReady app and service. Contact: support@velozready.com.

This notice is VelozReady's consumer health data privacy policy, including for the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law, and Connecticut's consumer health data provisions. It adds to our Privacy Policy. VelozReady is a training tool, not a health-care provider, so information in VelozReady is not protected by HIPAA. The laws listed in Section 10 still protect it.

1. Consumer health data we collect

"Consumer health data" means information that identifies you and relates to your physical or mental health. In VelozReady, that includes:

  • Check-in answers: effort and your one-tap "How did you feel?" or "How did it go?" answer. Check-ins saved before November 13, 2026 may also include earlier, more detailed answers (legs, breathing, energy, sleep, fueling, hydration, pain). VelozReady no longer asks about pain: if you are hurting, tell your coach or athletic trainer.
  • Training data: workouts, distance, duration, race results, and notes about them.
  • Calculated information: a readiness score and band, and trends. The score is calculated on your iPhone from your effort, how long you trained, how you felt, recent hard days, and, if Apple Health is connected, today's sleep, resting heart rate, and HRV compared with your own 30-day averages. Only the score is saved.
  • Apple Health summaries (optional): daily HRV, resting heart rate, sleep hours, walking and running distance, workout count, and latest workout details. These are created only for categories you share with a team.
  • Consent and sharing records for these categories.
  • Anything about your health you choose to include in a message to support@velozready.com.

VelozReady does not collect medical records, diagnoses, prescriptions, genetic or biometric identifiers, reproductive or sexual health information, or precise location.

2. Where it comes from

From you (your check-ins and messages), from Apple Health on your iPhone if you allow it, and from VelozReady's calculations based on your entries. We do not buy health data from anyone.

3. Why we collect and use it

We use consumer health data only to:

  • save your private training history and show you readiness feedback and trends;
  • show a coach the specific categories you choose to share with that coach's team;
  • keep records of your consent and choices;
  • keep the Service secure and investigate misuse; and
  • comply with law.

We never use consumer health data for advertising, never sell it, and never use it to train artificial-intelligence models. We never use it for insurance, employment, credit, or school-discipline decisions.

4. Your consent

When you create your account, you give this consent with its own separate checkbox. It is not bundled into the Terms. If you do not check it, you cannot create an account, because check-ins are the core of VelozReady. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account, or by emailing us to stop collection and delete your health data.

Connecting Apple Health is a second, optional permission that you give in Apple's own permission screen. You can use VelozReady without it.

Sharing with a coach is a third, separate choice. After you sign in, VelozReady asks once whether to share with your coaches. If you choose to share, the standard categories turn on for each team that approves you. Otherwise you choose for each team and each category. Joining a team shares nothing unless you made that choice, and you can change it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

  • Coaches you choose. Only the categories you turn on for that team, and only while you are on that team. Coaches see summaries, not raw answers. For example, they see a readiness band, never your exact score or what went into it. See the Team Sharing Notice for exactly what is and is not shown.
  • Your teammates, only in these limited ways. If you share race results with a team, your best 5K counts toward a team average 5K that teammates and alumni see only when at least three runners count toward it. If you turn on PR celebrations for a team, your new PRs may be posted on that team's board. Teammates and alumni never see your readiness, check-in answers, or Apple Health information.
  • Our service providers (processors). Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, storage, and server functions in the United States. Apple provides Apple Health on your device. They may process consumer health data only to provide their services to us.
  • When the law requires. For example, a valid court order, or reporting needed to protect someone's safety.

We do not share or sell consumer health data to anyone else. We do not let third parties collect it through VelozReady.

6. How Apple Health works in VelozReady

  • VelozReady only reads Apple Health data. It never writes to Apple Health and does not use background delivery.
  • Raw samples and live heart rate stay on your iPhone. VelozReady reads recent data (heart readings from the last 36 hours and workouts from the last 7 days) and calculates 30-day averages for resting heart rate and HRV on your iPhone. Those averages are never uploaded.
  • When you log a practice, today's Apple Health sleep, resting heart rate, and HRV help calculate that practice's readiness score on your iPhone. Only the score is saved.
  • A daily summary is sent to our servers only for a category you have turned on for at least one team. It syncs when you open the app.
  • Each team sees a category only from the moment you turned it on for that team.
  • You can stop Apple Health access at any time in the iPhone Settings app (Privacy & Security → Health → VelozReady).

7. Your rights

You may:

  • confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and get a list of everyone we shared it with;
  • access and get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it, including from our processors. You can delete individual check-ins or your whole account in the app, or email us to delete Apple Health summaries or other health data while keeping your account;
  • withdraw consent to collection or sharing; and
  • appeal if we deny a request.

Use the in-app controls or email support@velozready.com with the subject "Health Data Request." We confirm the request comes from your account's email address. We respond within 45 days (extendable once by 45 days if reasonably necessary, with notice). If we deny your request, reply with the subject "Health Data Appeal." We decide appeals within 45 days. If you disagree with the outcome, you may contact your state attorney general. Washington residents may submit a complaint to the Washington State Attorney General. Exercising these rights is free, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

8. Deletion and leaving a team

  • Deleting a check-in removes it right away.
  • Turning a category off or leaving a team ends that coach's access immediately. Your data stays in your own account.
  • Apple Health summaries already synced stay in your account until you delete your account or ask us to delete them.
  • Deleting your account permanently removes all of your consumer health data, and your team board posts, from our active systems immediately. Backup copies are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Withdrawal cannot undo what a coach already saw while sharing was on.

9. Security and breach notice

We protect consumer health data with encryption in transit and at rest, server-side permission checks for every coach request, per-team sharing controls, private storage, and restricted administrator access. If a breach affects your identifiable health information, we will notify you, the Federal Trade Commission, and other authorities as the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state laws require.

10. Laws that apply

  • Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373)
  • Nevada consumer health data law (Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 603A)
  • Connecticut Data Privacy Act consumer health data provisions
  • FTC Act Section 5 and FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 318)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (where it applies) and the California Online Privacy Protection Act
  • Florida Information Protection Act (Fla. Stat. § 501.171) and other state breach-notification laws
  • Apple's App Store rules for HealthKit data

11. Changes

We will not collect a new type of consumer health data or use it for a new purpose without updating this notice and getting your consent first. When a material change is made, the app will ask you to accept the new version.

12. Contact

Health data requests and appeals: support@velozready.com
