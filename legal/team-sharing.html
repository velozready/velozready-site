Version1.3
Last updatedNovember 14, 2026
Effective dateNovember 14, 2026

This notice explains what happens when an athlete joins a team on VelozReady and exactly what a coach can and cannot see.

1. Joining a team

  • A coach shares a private invitation code or link. Using it sends a request. The athlete is not on the team until a coach on that team approves the request.
  • Only coaches whose organization VelozReady has verified can approve requests or see athlete information.
  • Joining connects the athlete's personal account to the team. It does not give the team, coach, school, or club ownership of the account or the athlete's history.

2. Nothing is shared until the athlete chooses to share

Right after signing in, VelozReady asks each athlete once: "Share with your coach?"

  • Share with my coaches. Whenever a coach approves the athlete for a team, these categories turn on for that team automatically: profile identity, practice recaps, race results, readiness band, sleep summary, resting heart rate, HRV, and workout summaries. This also applies to teams the athlete is already on but has not chosen sharing for. VelozReady records a separate consent for each team.
  • Not now. Every sharing category starts off. After joining, the coach sees only the athlete's membership on the roster until the athlete chooses categories for that team.

Either way, the athlete can change any category for any team, or turn automatic sharing on or off, at any time in Profile → Teams & coach access. Turning automatic sharing off does not change teams the athlete is already sharing with. Those keep their current choices until the athlete changes them. A category the athlete turned off for a team is never turned back on automatically.

3. Categories an athlete can share

CategoryWhat the coach sees when it is on
Profile identityThe athlete's name or athlete code on the roster
Practice recapsPractice date, sport, workout, distance, duration, and effort (1–10)
Race resultsRace date, event, meet, and finish time
Readiness bandOne of four bands: Ready, Good, Watch, or Recovery First, from the score the athlete's iPhone calculated (effort, how they felt, and, if connected, Apple Health). Never the exact score or what went into it
Sleep summaryDaily sleep hours from Apple Health
Resting heart rateDaily resting heart rate from Apple Health
HRVDaily heart-rate variability from Apple Health
HRV device sourceWhich device recorded the HRV (requires HRV to be on)
Workout summariesDaily distance, workout count, and latest workout from Apple Health
Profile photoNot shown to coaches at this time
Coach messagesVelozReady does not offer messaging at this time; nothing is shared

Each Apple Health category also requires the athlete's Apple Health permission on their iPhone. Coaches see Apple Health summaries only from the moment the athlete turned that category on for that team, never earlier days.

4. What coaches never see

  • The exact readiness score, the athlete's "How did you feel?" answers, or any older detailed check-in answers (legs, breathing, energy, sleep, fueling, hydration, pain)
  • Private notes and workout screenshots
  • Raw Apple Health samples or live heart rate
  • The athlete's email address, password, sign-in details, or birth information
  • Any category the athlete has not turned on, and any history from before it was turned on
  • Anything at all after the athlete leaves or is removed from the team

5. Turning sharing off and leaving

  • Turning a category off ends that team's access to it immediately.
  • Leaving a team, or being removed by a coach, ends the coach's access to every category immediately. VelozReady records the withdrawal.
  • The athlete's own data stays in their personal account. Nothing is copied into the coach's account, so there is nothing left behind for the coach to view.
  • Information a coach already saw while sharing was on cannot be taken back. VelozReady cannot control notes or copies a coach made outside the app.
  • To rejoin, the athlete needs a new invitation and a new coach approval. Sharing for the rejoined team starts again only if automatic sharing is on, or when the athlete chooses categories again.

6. Coach responsibilities

Coaches must use athlete information only to coach that athlete on that team. They must respect each athlete's choices and must not sell, publish, or misuse it (see the Terms of Use, Section 6). Coaches are responsible for their own decisions about athletes.

7. Verification

A "verified organization" means VelozReady reviewed the coach's claimed affiliation with that organization, for example through an official email address or a public staff directory. It is not an endorsement, background check, license, or guarantee of the coach.

Questions or privacy requests: support@velozready.com
