This notice explains what happens when an athlete joins a team on VelozReady, exactly what a coach can and cannot see, and what the rest of the team sees.

1. Joining a team

  • A coach shares a private invitation code or link. Using it sends a request. The athlete is not on the team until a coach on that team approves the request.
  • A team's head coach belongs to an organization VelozReady has verified. Assistant coaches are added by that head coach and must be 18 or older. Only the team's head and assistant coaches can approve requests or see athlete information.
  • Joining connects the athlete's personal account to the team. It does not give the team, coach, school, or club ownership of the account or the athlete's history.

2. Nothing is shared until the athlete chooses to share

Right after signing in, VelozReady asks each athlete once: "Share with your coach?"

  • Share with my coaches. Whenever a coach approves the athlete for a team, these categories turn on for that team automatically: profile identity, practice recaps, race results, readiness band, sleep summary, resting heart rate, HRV, and workout summaries. This also applies to teams the athlete is already on but has not chosen sharing for. VelozReady records a separate consent for each team.
  • Not now. Every sharing category starts off. After joining, the coach sees only the athlete's membership on the roster until the athlete chooses categories for that team.

Either way, the athlete can change any category for any team, or turn automatic sharing on or off, at any time in Settings → Teams & sharing. Turning automatic sharing off does not change teams the athlete is already sharing with. Those keep their current choices until the athlete changes them. A category the athlete turned off for a team is never turned back on automatically.

3. Categories an athlete can share

CategoryWhat the coach sees when it is on
Profile identityThe athlete's name or athlete code on the roster
Practice recapsPractice date, sport, workout, distance, duration, and effort (1–10)
Race resultsRace date, event, meet, and finish time
Readiness bandOne of four bands: Ready, Good, Watch, or Recovery First, from the score the athlete's iPhone calculated (effort, how they felt, and, if connected, Apple Health). Never the exact score or what went into it
Sleep summaryDaily sleep hours from Apple Health
Resting heart rateDaily resting heart rate from Apple Health
HRVDaily heart-rate variability from Apple Health
Workout summariesDaily distance, workout count, and latest workout from Apple Health

The profile photo is not shared with anyone at this time.

Each Apple Health category also requires the athlete's Apple Health permission on their iPhone. Coaches see Apple Health summaries only from the moment the athlete turned that category on for that team, never earlier days.

3A. The team board, calendar, and team tiles

  • Team board. Coaches post announcements and workouts, and everyone on the team can chat (the head coach can turn chat off). Everything on the board is visible to the whole team, including coaches and alumni. There are no private or direct messages. Each person can report posts and block people. See the Terms of Use, Section 7A.
  • PR celebrations (optional, per team). If an athlete turns on "Celebrate my PRs with the team," coaches and alumni may post the athlete's new PR (event, time, improvement, and meet) on the board. It is off until the athlete turns it on, and turning it off or leaving the team stops it.
  • Team tiles. Coaches see a team average 5K and a count of PRs this week, built from athletes who share race results with the team. Athletes and alumni see the average only when at least three runners count toward it, so no one's time can be worked out, and see a PR count only from teammates who turned on PR celebrations.
  • Calendar and logo. Coaches add meets, practices, and events, and the head coach can add a team logo. Everyone on the team sees them.

3B. Alumni

Alumni are former team members, 18 or older, invited by the head coach to help on the team board. They can post and pin on the board. They never see any athlete's shared practices, races, readiness, or Apple Health information.

3C. What coaches record at practice and meets

Coaches may use VelozReady's coach tools to run practices and meets. What a coach records there is created by the coach, not shared by the athlete, so it does not depend on the sharing choices in Section 2.

  • What coaches may record: attendance, workout groups and assigned workouts, practice times and splits (for example, reps, time trials, and relays), and meet entries and relay order.
  • Who sees it: the team's coaches, and each athlete for their own records. Teammates and alumni see it only when a coach shares it with the team, such as a meet lineup or results posted on the board.
  • What it never includes: anything from the athlete's own check-ins, readiness, or Apple Health. Coaches see those only through the sharing categories in Section 3.
  • When an athlete leaves: coaches no longer see that athlete's records. Times a coach recorded stay in the athlete's own history. Board posts that already included them stay until a coach or the author deletes them.

4. What coaches never see

  • The exact readiness score, the athlete's "How did you feel?" answers, or any older detailed check-in answers (legs, breathing, energy, sleep, fueling, hydration, pain)
  • Private notes and workout screenshots
  • Raw Apple Health samples or live heart rate
  • The athlete's email address, password, sign-in details, or birth information
  • Any category the athlete has not turned on, and any history from before it was turned on
  • Anything at all after the athlete leaves or is removed from the team

Teammates and alumni never see any of an athlete's sharing categories. They see only the athlete's board posts, PR celebrations the athlete opted into, team totals as described in Section 3A, and results or lineups a coach shares with the team (Section 3C).

5. Turning sharing off and leaving

  • Turning a category off ends that team's access to it immediately.
  • Leaving a team, or being removed by a coach, ends the coach's access to every category immediately. VelozReady records the withdrawal.
  • The athlete's own data stays in their personal account. Nothing is copied into the coach's account, so there is nothing left behind for the coach to view.
  • Information a coach already saw while sharing was on cannot be taken back. VelozReady cannot control notes or copies a coach made outside the app.
  • To rejoin, the athlete needs a new invitation and a new coach approval. Sharing for the rejoined team starts again only if automatic sharing is on, or when the athlete chooses categories again.

6. Coach responsibilities

Coaches must use athlete information only to coach that athlete on that team. They must respect each athlete's choices and must not sell, publish, or misuse it (see the Terms of Use, Section 6). Coaches are responsible for their own decisions about athletes and for moderating their team's board. A coach may give an athlete a roster name (for example, to tell two athletes with the same name apart); only that team's coaches see it.

7. Verification

A "verified organization" means VelozReady reviewed the coach's claimed affiliation with that organization, for example through an official email address or a public staff directory. It is not an endorsement, background check, license, or guarantee of the coach.
